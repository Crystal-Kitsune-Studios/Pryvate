Privacy is complicated. This part shouldn't be.

Does this automatically remove my data?

Not yet. This page creates a request for you to review and send. It doesn't find your records, contact companies, or track responses.

Where does my information go?

The builder runs in your browser and does not send or save your entries. If you copy the draft or open your email app, you choose where to share it.

Will every company delete my data?

Not necessarily. Rights and exceptions depend on where you live and the company. Some businesses use specific forms or may need reasonable verification before acting.

Is Pryvate open source?

Pryvate is being envisioned as an open-source project. The public repository and license are still to be announced.
